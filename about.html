About Basketball Arena

A free, instant 3D basketball shooting game plus a basketball blog - made for fans, by a fan.

Who Runs This Site

Basketball Arena is created and maintained by Dhyey Satani, a basketball fan and web developer. The project is built as a hobby to share a fun, polished basketball experience with players around the world - no downloads, no sign-ups, no paywalls.

Questions, feedback or partnership ideas? Visit the contact page and we will get back to you.

What This Site Provides

  • The game: Basketball Arena is a free 3D arcade basketball shooting game that runs right in your browser using WebGL. It features 7 game modes (Arcade, Time Attack, Moving Hoop, Trick Shot, Challenge, Hard Mode and Practice), combo scoring, Fire Mode multipliers, ball skins and global leaderboards.
  • The blog: Our blog publishes practical basketball tips, strategies, drills and guides for players of every level, along with coverage of baseball and esports news.

Why We Built It

We love basketball and we love well-made games. Too many "free" online games are full of ads, sign-ups and bloat. We wanted a clean, fast, genuinely free alternative that respects your time - just open the page and start shooting in seconds.

How the Site Is Funded

The site is free for everyone. To cover hosting and development costs, we display advertising through Google AdSense. We never sell your personal data. You can read more in our Privacy Policy.
