FISSURE Playground 3: Teams, Format, Schedule and Prizes

FISSURE Playground 3 is back, and the latest installment promises bigger stakes, a refreshed format, and plenty of action for CS2 fans around the world.

Category: Esports News Published: September 6, 2026 Updated: September 6, 2026 3 min read

What is FISSURE Playground 3?

FISSURE Playground 3 is the third edition of the popular tournament series organized by FISSURE, a tournament organizer that has steadily built a reputation for hosting high-quality Counter-Strike events. Building on the success of the first two iterations, this edition aims to deliver an even more competitive and spectator-friendly experience.

The tournament brings together a mix of established tier-one teams and rising challengers, creating a dynamic environment where upsets are always possible. For fans of competitive Counter-Strike, FISSURE Playground has become a reliable source of thrilling matches, innovative production, and storylines that carry throughout the season.

FISSURE Playground continues to carve out a space where rising teams can test themselves against the best, and where fans get to witness the next chapter of competitive Counter-Strike unfold in real time.

Teams competing at FISSURE Playground 3

The team list for FISSURE Playground 3 features a compelling blend of top-ranked rosters and hungry underdogs. The organizers have invited several well-known organizations while also leaving room for teams that earned their spot through qualifiers and regional pathways.

The diversity of the team pool is one of the things that makes FISSURE Playground stand out. Fans get to see how established stars handle the pressure of facing teams that have nothing to lose, which often produces some of the most memorable moments of the tournament.

Tournament format explained

FISSURE Playground 3 uses a multi-stage format designed to reward consistency while still giving teams a chance to recover from early setbacks. The tournament typically begins with a group stage, where teams are divided into groups and play a round-robin or Swiss-style series of matches.

From the group stage, the top-performing teams advance to the playoff bracket, which is played in a single-elimination or double-elimination format depending on the specific rules for this edition. The playoffs are where the tension reaches its peak, as every match carries elimination consequences and a single bad game can end a team's tournament run.

The format has been praised by fans and analysts alike for striking a balance between fairness and excitement. Teams that perform well in the group stage earn advantages in the playoff bracket, but the door is never completely closed for a team that starts slow and finds its rhythm later in the event.

Schedule and key dates

The schedule for FISSURE Playground 3 spans several days of intense Counter-Strike action. The tournament is structured to give fans a steady stream of matches without overwhelming them, with group stage matches typically running across the first few days followed by playoff matches that build toward a grand final.

Fans are encouraged to check the official FISSURE channels and HLTV for the most up-to-date schedule information, as match times can shift based on the results of earlier rounds and any logistical adjustments made by the organizers.

Prize pool and fantasy options

The prize pool for FISSURE Playground 3 is a significant draw for competing teams. While the exact figure varies by edition, FISSURE has consistently offered competitive payouts that make the tournament attractive to both established organizations and smaller teams looking to secure meaningful earnings.

In addition to the standard prize distribution, the tournament also features a fantasy component that allows fans to engage with the event on a deeper level. The fantasy system lets fans build their own rosters using players from the competing teams, earning points based on real-world performance throughout the tournament.
